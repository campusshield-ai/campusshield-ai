07 · TRUST & PRIVACY CENTRE

Show who should see what.

Explore example access scenarios to understand why student identity, private incident reports and general community posts should not all be visible to the same people.

Private incident report

Student: can submit and follow the report according to the platform workflow. Authorised campus personnel: can review the information needed for their assigned response. Other students and public visitors: should not see the private report or its evidence.

Identity review

Verification evidence should be submitted through a designated private process and reviewed by authorised personnel.

Role-based access

Capabilities should follow approved roles and campus membership, not a self-selected job title or public profile.

Human oversight

AI assistance and automated workflows should not replace accountable campus decisions or emergency procedures.
