CampusShield AI Help Centre

Quick guidance for getting started and using the platform responsibly.

Creating an account and signing in

Use the account form on the main page to sign in or create an account. Enter accurate information and follow any email-confirmation instructions shown to you. Never share your password, authentication codes, or recovery links with anyone.

Student identity verification

If identity verification is required, follow the instructions in your account and submit documents only through the platform's approved private upload flow. Do not send student ID documents by ordinary email or upload them to public links. Your institution may need to review your submission before protected features become available.

Reporting an incident

Provide clear, factual details and choose the most appropriate category and severity. Avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal information. Access to view or manage reports depends on your account role and the institution's configured permissions.

Access and account problems

If you cannot sign in, check that your email address is correct, look for account-confirmation messages, and use the available password-recovery options. If access remains blocked or your verification status seems incorrect, contact support using the official details on our Contact page.

Emergencies and urgent danger

CampusShield AI does not contact emergency services. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number or official campus security directly. Do not wait for a platform report or AI response.

Privacy and terms

Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use to understand the published information about platform use and data handling.
