WELCOME

One place for campus safety, information and student life

CampusShield AI brings together incident reporting, campus information, student communities, academic support and communication. What you can access depends on your account, verified campus membership, role, subscription and the features enabled by your university.

1. Create an account

Register with your email address, confirm it when prompted and sign in.

2. Complete your profile

Add your display name and keep your account information accurate.

3. Select and verify your campus

Choose your university in CampusPulse. Follow the private identity-verification flow if asked.

4. Explore the workspace

Use the sidebar, mobile navigation, Quick Actions or in-app “How to use CampusShield” guide.

SAFETY & RESPONSE

Report and follow up on an incident

Open Report incident from Quick Actions or the sidebar. Select the appropriate category and severity, enter a clear location and factual description, review your details, then submit. Use the status area to follow updates made available to your account.

Only authorized campus security or administrator accounts can access incident-management tools. Do not post sensitive incident details in CampusPulse, public comments or chat.

CAMPUS LIFE

CampusPulse, groups, events and services

CampusPulse

Choose your university to access the community feed, groups and student-life features enabled for your campus.

Events & noticeboard

Browse campus events, official notices and available student services. Check the source and date of each notice.

Lost & Found

Share or search for item notices. Do not include private identity details or sensitive personal information.

Helpdesk & contacts

Use Campus service contacts for available university offices. Use Helpdesk for non-emergency requests when enabled.

STUDENT-LED CHANGE

Ideas & Impact: discover proposals and share improvements

Every signed-in CampusShield member can browse approved ideas from participating universities. Idea cards show the creator's CampusPulse username, the problem, proposed solution and expected impact.

  • Active, verified campus members with a CampusPulse username can submit ideas, support published proposals and comment.
  • New ideas remain visible only to their author and authorized moderators at the originating university until approved.
  • Verified staff moderation is limited to the staff member's own university.

COMMUNICATION

Campus Chat, Private Messages and voice notes

Use Campus Chat for the campus conversation space and Private Messages for one-to-one conversations. When the microphone control is available, grant browser microphone permission to record a voice note. Check the recipient before sending; private voice notes are restricted to conversation participants.

WEBSITE & MOBILE APP

Install CampusShield on your device

  1. Open CampusShield in a supported browser.
  2. Select the Install app button when it appears, or use your browser's Add to Home Screen or Install app menu.
  3. Follow the prompt, then open CampusShield from your home screen and sign in.
  4. Reconnect to the internet and reload to receive website updates.

CampusShield is designed to work across desktop, tablet and mobile screens. Signing in, loading campus data, submitting reports, messaging and other live features require an internet connection. The offline page is only a connection fallback; reports are not saved for later offline submission.

ROLE-BASED ACCESS

Why do some people see different tools?

Students

Use student-life, academic, reporting and community tools allowed by their account and campus verification.

Teachers and campus staff

Verified staff may receive classroom, review or campus-management access where enabled.

Security staff

Authorized security accounts can access incident-management features within their assigned permissions.

Administrators

Verified administrators may access approvals, administration and additional management tools.

USE CAMPUSSHIELD RESPONSIBLY

Privacy, account safety and emergencies

  • Never share passwords, one-time codes or recovery links.
  • Upload identity documents only through the private verification flow—not ordinary chat or public posts.
  • Do not put confidential incident, health or attendance information into public community posts or AI prompts.
  • Review the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
Immediate danger? Contact your campus security control room or local emergency services directly. CampusShield AI does not automatically call, dispatch or alert emergency services. Do not wait for a platform report, chat reply or AI response.
