**Prepared:** 2026-10-31

**Campus shown:** Babcock University Main Campus, Ilishan-Remo, Ogun State  
**Evidence type:** User-provided screenshots of the StudentHub interactive map in 2D, satellite and 3D modes.

## Purpose and confidence boundary

The screenshots provide a useful visual and naming reference for the broad arrangement of the Main Campus. They show labelled map locations, road/path geometry, satellite imagery and a 3D-style presentation.

They are not an approved university site plan or a georeferenced survey. Screenshot labels and marker positions must therefore be stored as **reference leads**, not as verified campus features. Do not derive exact coordinates, building footprints, entrances, heights, campus boundaries or pedestrian routing from screenshot pixels.

The separate architectural aerial image supplied with the screenshots is approved by the project owner as a **visual-design reference** for a brighter, landscaped, realistic 3D campus presentation. It is illustrative only; it is not evidence of Babcock's actual architecture, building positions or site layout. Do not trace it or present it as a photograph/render of the real campus.

## Layout observations (qualitative only)

- The Main Gate and Senate/SAT area appear toward the lower/south-western portion of the displayed campus view.
- The Amphitheatre, library, cafeteria and several student facilities appear around the central portion.
- Many residence labels appear across the northern/central portion.
- The University Stadium appears toward the north-eastern portion.
- The Water Plant and Sapphire Hall labels appear toward the eastern/south-eastern portion.
- BU Teaching Hospital and the staff/high-school labels appear toward the western/south-western side.

These are relative visual observations from one map viewport, not surveyed directions or a verified campus plan.

## Recommended CampusShield AI treatment

1. Keep StudentHub as a secondary community-map reference and preserve its URL as provenance.
2. Reconcile every proposed canonical name with official Babcock pages (residence pages, library, new-student information, facilities and other relevant university departments).
3. Discover candidate OSM building polygons only after the exact campus-centre and boundary workflow permits it. Preserve OSM object IDs, source URLs and required ODbL attribution.
4. Match names to footprints only with reliable evidence such as an approved university plan, permitted survey or verified campus-admin inspection.
5. Keep candidate labels, points and footprints private and pending review. Never create coordinates by converting screenshot pixel positions.
6. Keep building entrances separate from polygon centroids. Do not generate walking routes, emergency routes, accessible routes or crossings from visual guesses.
7. Treat the approved architectural image as styling inspiration only: natural daylight, realistic building massing, landscaped open spaces, clear campus roads and pedestrian paths, restrained labels and a readable 2D/satellite/3D mode switch. Real campus geometry and imagery must come from valid map sources, not from the concept image.
