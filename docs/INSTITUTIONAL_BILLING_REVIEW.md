# Institutional subscription requests and finance review

## Purpose

CampusShield keeps essential student safety access free. University subscriptions fund institution-level administration, analytics, moderation, integrations and support. The institutional billing form is an enquiry and procurement workflow; it is not a checkout and it does not activate paid access.

## Request journey

1. A verified campus administrator selects Starter, Professional or Enterprise and a monthly or annual term.
2. The request is stored with the authenticated requester, institution name, contact email, requested plan and optional context.
3. A trusted CampusShield platform administrator reviews the request in **Platform administration → Institutional billing review**.
4. The reviewer can mark the enquiry pending, contacted, approved for further procurement, or not approved. A response message can be sent back to the requesting university.
5. An approved enquiry still requires a separate invoice, written agreement and confirmed payment.
6. Payment reconciliation and subscription activation remain separate controlled operations. This review queue never writes payment records or entitlements.

## Status meanings

- **Pending review** — no decision has been recorded yet.
- **Contacted** — CampusShield has begun the conversation with the institution.
- **Request approved** — the enquiry may proceed to procurement and invoice preparation; payment is still outstanding and no features are unlocked.
- **Not approved** — CampusShield is not proceeding with this request.

The public response message is visible only to the requester and trusted platform administrators.

## Campus scope

A billing request is tied to the canonical university ID from the signed-in account's campus membership. The form pre-fills and locks the official university name from that verified campus context. The database independently requires the requester to have a verified profile plus an active, verified membership whose campus role is `admin`, and it verifies that the submitted university ID/name match that membership. Typing another university name cannot redirect the request.

## Security boundary

- Only verified campus administrators can submit and read their own institutional requests under the existing request policies.
- Only user IDs registered as trusted platform administrators by the database owner can read the global queue or update request status and response message.
- Authenticated update permission is column-scoped to `status` and `response_message`; ordinary users have no update policy.
- The database records the trusted reviewer and review timestamp when the status or response changes.
- No service-role credential is placed in browser code.
- The feature does not change student identity verification, university memberships, campus roles, or safety access.

## Invoice issuance

After a request is marked approved, a trusted platform administrator can issue one invoice for that request from **Platform administration → Institutional billing review**.

- Invoice numbers are generated by the database and are unique.
- The invoice bill-to institution, recipient email, plan and billing period are copied from the approved request by a database trigger; the browser cannot substitute another institution.
- Finance confirms the actual quoted subtotal, due date and verified payment instructions before issuing. The Starter and Professional amounts are suggested from the published price list; Enterprise is only a starting quote and must be confirmed.
- Tax is entered as an explicit amount by finance. The system does not silently assume a VAT rate or calculate a tax amount.
- Invoice totals are calculated in the database in kobo, with NGN as the currency.
- The requesting verified campus administrator can see their own invoice history and use **Print invoice / Save PDF** to print or save a PDF from the browser. Trusted platform admins can print from the finance queue. The printable document is currently a **pro forma invoice/payment request**, not a receipt or final statutory tax invoice; CampusShield's registered legal issuer name, address and tax registration details must be configured before final tax invoices are issued.
- An issued pro forma invoice is immutable in this phase. The related request cannot be moved to a different review status after issuance. A correction/void-and-reissue workflow is a separate future phase.

An invoice means **amount due**, not amount received. The invoice stays issued until a separate verified payment-reconciliation workflow changes the payment state. Issuing an invoice never activates paid campus features.

## Institutional Paystack payment-page links

The account owner supplied three hosted Paystack subscription-page URLs:

| Plan | Fixed monthly page amount | Payment page |
|---|---:|---|
| Starter University | ₦100,000 | https://paystack.shop/pay/gqjub0yz1y |
| Professional University | ₦250,000 | https://paystack.shop/pay/8u81ku5v94 |
| Enterprise University (base only) | ₦500,000 | https://paystack.shop/pay/p1h6s3mgcj |

The pricing-card buttons continue to start an institutional billing request, not an immediate public checkout. After finance approves the request and issues an invoice, the requester sees a Paystack action only when all of these match: the invoice is still `issued`, its currency is NGN, its term is monthly, its plan is one of the three fixed tiers, and its total (including tax) equals that exact page amount.

Annual invoices are not sent to monthly pages. Enterprise custom quotes and any invoice with a tax/total adjustment are not sent to the fixed ₦500,000 link. They must use the separately approved payment instructions on the invoice.

**These are recurring subscription pages, not one-time invoice checkout pages.** Paystack documents that a subscription page starts a recurring subscription after the customer pays. If its invoice limit is left blank, Paystack continues charging at the set interval until the subscription is cancelled. Institutions must expressly authorise recurring monthly debits; do not describe this as a one-time payment. Before publishing links for live collection, finance must confirm the merchant's Test/Live mode, the plan amount/interval and the recurring/cancellation policy.

The hosted page does not receive the CampusShield invoice ID as metadata. A successful redirect or customer-facing success message is not payment verification. Finance must retain the Paystack transaction/subscription reference and reconcile it against the issued invoice. Leaving the Paystack page does not mark the invoice paid or activate entitlements. A separate signed institutional webhook is available for Staging, but it must be configured in the Paystack Test Mode dashboard; it records events only and does not activate subscriptions.

## Institutional payment verification and activation

Staging now includes a separate institutional Paystack event inbox and finance verification path. It does not reuse ShieldMind's personal subscription tables or webhook processing.

- The institutional webhook verifies Paystack's `x-paystack-signature` using HMAC SHA-512 before storing selected event fields in a private event inbox. It captures `charge.success`, subscription create/disable/not-renew events, invoice updates/failures and dispute/refund signals. The webhook is an evidence collector only; it never changes invoice or subscription status.
- Trusted platform finance administrators can enter the Paystack transaction reference against an issued invoice in **Platform administration → Institutional billing review** and select **Verify & activate**.
- The backend checks the invoice is issued and monthly, then calls Paystack's server-side Verify Transaction API. It requires a successful test-domain transaction, exact NGN amount, and a customer email matching the invoice bill-to email. A browser redirect or customer success message is not accepted as proof of payment.
- A single database transaction records the invoice as paid, records the subscription, and appends a finance audit entry with the verifying administrator, transaction reference, amount, currency and Paystack payment date. Repeated references and references already used for another invoice are rejected.
- The created institutional subscription is readable only by trusted platform administrators and verified active campus administrators for that university. The requester billing page displays the recorded plan and expiry.

**Current limit:** this activation action handles the initial fixed-price monthly payment only: Starter ₦100,000, Professional ₦250,000 and Enterprise base ₦500,000. Annual invoices, custom Enterprise totals and tax-adjusted totals are not eligible for these links. The activation period recorded is one calendar month. Later recurring Paystack charges, failed renewals, cancellations, refunds and disputes are captured as events but do not yet extend or revoke the CampusShield subscription automatically. Do not publish the monthly subscription pages for live collection until the recurring renewal and cancellation workflow is completed and tested. The Paystack page itself can continue charging monthly while CampusShield's current activation period expires.
