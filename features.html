BEYOND THE BASICS

A connected campus experience

CampusShield is designed to bring everyday campus tools closer together. Individual modules may be enabled differently by each institution.

CampusPulse & student communities

Discover campus conversations, groups and student-led activity in the spaces enabled for your institution.

Private Messages

Use one-to-one conversations and message requests for more focused communication. Keep sensitive safety details out of ordinary chat.

CampusMap

Explore campus geography and available place information. Building coverage and verified details depend on the map data available for each campus.

Academics & exam preparation

Organise timetable information, course communities and available study resources. Practice material should be checked against your course requirements.

Campus services & noticeboard

Find campus notices, events and service information where the relevant university sources and modules are enabled.

Ideas, opportunities & discovery

Participate in Ideas & Impact and explore opportunities, marketplace listings and Lost & Found when available to your campus.

Feature availability can vary by institution, account role, and deployment configuration. CampusShield AI is a demonstration platform and does not contact emergency services.
