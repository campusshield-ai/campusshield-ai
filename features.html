TEN PLATFORM AREAS

Explore the CampusShield ecosystem

A connected set of campus tools for safety workflows, student communities, academic support and everyday services. Availability depends on campus configuration and account permissions.

01 · SAFETY

Incident reporting

Submit a campus safety concern with relevant details so authorised personnel can review it and follow up through the configured incident workflow.

02 · SAFETY

Incident management

Authorised security users can review submitted reports, update available status fields and coordinate follow-up in line with their campus permissions.

03 · CAMPUS UPDATES

Campus alerts & noticeboard

Find available campus announcements, notices and updates. The platform does not automatically contact or dispatch emergency services.

04 · DISCOVERY

CampusMap

Explore campus geography and available place information. Building coverage, entrances and location accuracy depend on the map data available and verified for each campus.

05 · INSIGHTS

Risk analysis & safety insights

Review available incident patterns and campus risk information to support awareness and planning. Insights depend on the quality and completeness of the underlying data.

06 · COMMUNITY

CampusPulse & student communities

Discover campus conversations, groups and student-led activity in the community spaces enabled for your institution. Keep sensitive personal or safety information out of public posts.

07 · COMMUNICATION

Private Messages

Use one-to-one conversations and message requests for focused communication. Private messages are not a substitute for the incident-reporting or official emergency process.

08 · AI SUPPORT

ShieldMind AI

Where configured and available, the campus assistant can help users explore campus knowledge, understand academic topics and organise learning. Check important answers against official sources.

09 · ACADEMICS

Academic workspace

Bring together timetable information, course communities, study resources and past-question practice where the relevant modules and materials are enabled for your campus.

10 · SERVICES

Student services & discovery

Find available events, campus service information, Helpdesk, Lost & Found, marketplace listings and student opportunities in the modules enabled for your institution.

DESIGNED AROUND CAMPUS ROLES

One platform, different campus experiences

For students

Discover campus communities, available services, academic resources, timetable information and ways to submit campus concerns.

For campus security

Use authorised incident review, status management and available risk information to support established campus safety procedures.

For university administrators

Support campus membership review, role-aware access and institutional information through the configuration and permissions assigned to your institution.

GETTING STARTED

How users enter the campus experience

1. Create an account

Provide account details and select your institution through the CampusShield sign-up flow.

2. Complete verification

Submit student or staff evidence through the designated private workflow where required. Review is handled by authorised personnel.

3. Use enabled tools

Access features according to your approved account role, verified campus membership and institution configuration.

Feature availability can vary by institution, account role, and deployment configuration. CampusShield AI is a demonstration platform and does not contact emergency services.
