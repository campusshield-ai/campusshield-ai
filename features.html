CampusShield AI Features

One connected experience for campus safety, trusted access, and student life.

Incident reporting

Submit campus safety concerns with relevant details so authorized campus personnel can review and follow up according to their access permissions.

Incident management

Support authorized security staff with incident review, status updates, and organized follow-up workflows.

Risk analysis

Review available incident patterns and campus risk information to support awareness and informed planning. Analytics depend on the data available to the institution.

Identity verification

Support institution-specific identity checks and administrator review for protected student features. Identity documents should be submitted only through the approved private workflow.

Campus community and opportunities

Where enabled, discover campus groups, events, notices, internships, scholarships, jobs, and other student opportunities.

ShieldMind AI and support

Where available and configured, the campus assistant can help users navigate campus information and platform resources. AI responses should be checked against official sources.

Feature availability can vary by institution, account role, and deployment configuration. CampusShield AI is a demonstration platform and does not contact emergency services.
