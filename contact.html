What to include in a support message

Where it happened

Name the page or feature, the device and browser you were using, and the approximate time of the issue.

What you expected

Describe the action you tried, what you expected to happen and what happened instead. Include the exact error text where possible.

Keep it safe

Remove student IDs, passwords, one-time codes, access tokens, payment details and private incident information from screenshots or messages.

Safety: CampusShield AI does not automatically contact or dispatch emergency services. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your campus security control room or local emergency services directly.
